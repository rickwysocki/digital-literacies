\begin{frame}[label={sec:org2e24ae4}]{Content Strategy - Processes}
According to Clark, content strategy:
\begin{itemize}
\item Integrates content, organizations, and its members at all stages of writing.
\item Aligns with authorial/organizational goals.
\item Emphasizes collaborative writing and knowledge.
\item Offers \alert{repeatable} workflows.
\item Develops new understandings of methods and tools (65).
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
